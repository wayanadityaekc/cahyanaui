Balinese Hinduism & Daily Offerings

The philosophy of harmony behind Bali's temples, ceremonies, and canang sari.

Bali Guide › People & Culture

Most Balinese follow a distinct island form of Hinduism, blended over centuries with local beliefs. At its heart is Tri Hita Karana - the idea that wellbeing comes from harmony between people, nature, and the divine.

You'll see this philosophy everywhere, most visibly in the canang sari: small palm-leaf trays of flowers and incense placed at homes, shops, and shrines each morning as a daily thank-you to the gods.

Temples, from tiny family shrines to grand sea temples, anchor community life, and the calendar is filled with colourful ceremonies year-round.

Good to Know

  • Canang sari = daily offerings you'll see everywhere
  • Thousands of temples across the island
  • Ceremonies happen all year round

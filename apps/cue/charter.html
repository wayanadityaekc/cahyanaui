How a Charter Day Works

A charter is the simplest way to see Bali on your own terms: one air-conditioned car, one local driver, and your plan. Choose a half day (5 hours, roughly a 60 km range) or a full day (10 hours, roughly 120 km), and the car is yours - petrol, parking, and the driver are all included, priced per car for up to 5 passengers.

There's no fixed route. Hand your driver a wishlist in the morning, or decide as you go - stop for a photo when a rice terrace appears, pull over for lunch at a warung, stay longer wherever you're happy. Our drivers know the island well and are glad to suggest routes, shortcuts, and places to eat along the way.

If the day runs long, no problem: extend hour by hour at the rate shown in the calculator above, decided on the day. Entrance tickets and meals stay in your hands, so you only spend on what you actually visit.

Charter or Guided Tour?

Both come with a private car and driver - the difference is structure. Our day tours follow a curated route with a local guide and a Standard / Exclusive ticket option, so the whole day is designed for you. A charter is pure freedom: transport, driver, and time, with the itinerary left entirely to you. If it's your first visit, a tour makes a great introduction; once you know what you love, a charter lets you chase it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is included in the charter price?

The car, petrol, parking, and your English-speaking driver - everything the car needs is covered. The price is per car for up to 5 passengers, not per person. Entrance tickets and meals are not included, so you only pay for the places you choose to visit.

Where can you pick us up?

Anywhere in Ubud and nearby at no extra cost. Pick-up from other areas of Bali works too - select your area in the calculator above and a small surcharge is added automatically, so the price you see is the price you pay.

What happens if we need the car longer?

Just say so on the day - the charter extends hour by hour at the hourly rate shown in the calculator. No pressure to decide upfront; plenty of guests add an hour when a sunset is too good to leave.

How much luggage fits in the car?

A standard car comfortably takes 5 passengers with normal luggage. Big bags, surfboards, or a bigger group? Tell us when you book and we'll send a larger vehicle.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Build your charter above, tap Book This Charter, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures the car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

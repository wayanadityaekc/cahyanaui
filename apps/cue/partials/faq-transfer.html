Transfer FAQ

Is the transfer price per person or per car?

Per car - one fixed price for up to 5 passengers, no matter how many of you travel. The rate for each route is shown upfront, so there are no meters, no surge pricing, and no haggling at the roadside.

Do you track flight delays for airport pick-ups?

Yes. Give us your flight number and we monitor it - if your plane is early or late, your driver adjusts. You won't be charged extra for a delay, and someone will be waiting for you with a sign when you land - pick-ups run around the clock, middle-of-the-night landings included.

How much luggage fits in the car?

A standard car comfortably fits up to 5 passengers with normal luggage. Travelling with lots of bags, surfboards, or a big group? Just tell us when you book and we'll send a larger vehicle so nothing gets left behind.

Where exactly do you meet me at the airport?

Your driver waits in the arrivals hall just outside customs, holding a sign with your name. We'll send you their name, photo, and phone number the day before, so you know exactly who you're looking for.

What if I need to cancel or change the time?

Just message us on WhatsApp as early as you can. Since transfers only need a small deposit - or nothing at all if you're staying at one of our villas - changing or cancelling with reasonable notice is free and easy.

Still have questions?

Message our local team on WhatsApp - we usually reply within a few hours.

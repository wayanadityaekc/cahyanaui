Bali Travel Guide

Everything worth knowing before you go - the island and its regions, the culture, the landscapes, what to do, and the practical bits that make a trip smooth.

About the Island

Cultural hub

Ubud

Bali's cultural heart in the cool green interior - temples, rice terraces, art, and yoga. The best base for day trips across the island.
Surf & cafes

Canggu

A laid-back coastal district of surf breaks, black-sand beaches, and a buzzing cafe scene. Livelier than Ubud, more relaxed than Kuta.
Clifftop south

Uluwatu & the Bukit

Dramatic limestone cliffs, world-class surf, and a famous clifftop sunset temple. Home to beaches like Padang Padang and Melasti.

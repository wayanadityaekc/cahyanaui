East Bali Tour

The wild, regal side of the island - the Gates of Heaven at Lempuyang, the royal water palace of Tirta Gangga, the grand pools of Taman Ujung, and Bali's holiest temple on the slopes of Mount Agung.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Lempuyang Temple - Gates of Heaven

Bali's most photographed split gate, framing Mount Agung on a clear morning. We arrive early to beat the queue, and your guide will handle the ticket, sarong, and the famous mirror photo so you don't waste half the day waiting in line.
Stop 2

Tirta Gangga Water Palace

A former royal garden built around tiered fountains and koi-filled pools, with stepping stones you can walk across the water on. Peaceful, green, and endlessly photogenic - one of East Bali's most underrated stops.
Stop 3

Taman Ujung Water Palace

The grand water palace of the Karangasem royal family - a spread of reflecting ponds, ornate bridges, and hillside pavilions framed by Mount Agung and the sea. Elegant, photogenic, and an easy stroll - a perfect pairing with Tirta Gangga.
Stop 4

Besakih - The Mother Temple

Bali's largest and holiest temple complex, built in terraces up the slope of Mount Agung. More than 20 temples in one sanctuary, still the spiritual centre of the island after a thousand years. Sarong and guide included.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes - every departure is 100% private: your own air-conditioned car with your own English-speaking driver & local guide, never shared with other guests. The price is per car for up to 5 people; groups of 6 or more simply take a second car.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

You choose between two versions with the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard covers the private car, petrol, and your crew - entrance tickets are paid at each stop, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. Exclusive adds entrance tickets for all the listed attractions for everyone in your group, so the whole day is prepaid and you never queue at a ticket window.

How long is the tour, and when does it start?

Plan for up to 10 hours - East Bali is a long, scenic drive from Ubud. Since the car is yours, the start time is up to you - most guests leave Ubud in the morning, and we agree the exact pick-up time when you book.

Can we change or skip stops?

Absolutely - the listed stops are a starting point, not a fixed route. Add a stop, swap one out, or stay longer wherever you're enjoying yourself; just tell us before the day or tell your driver on the road.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Tap the Book button, pick your date, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures your car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

How does the Gates of Heaven photo work?

The famous mirror shot at Lempuyang has a photo queue that grows through the day, which is why we start early. Your guide handles the ticket, sarong, and the photo line for you, and an optional fast pass for the photo queue is available if you want to skip the wait.

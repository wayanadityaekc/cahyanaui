North Bali Tour

The green, quiet north - twin crater lakes, the tallest waterfall on the island, mountain coffee villages, and dolphins breaking the water off Lovina at sunrise. Fewer tourists, bigger landscapes.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Twin Lakes Viewpoint (Buyan & Tamblingan)

Two crater lakes sitting side by side in an old volcanic caldera, separated by a thin strip of jungle. The viewpoint on the ridge above gives you both in one frame - best in the morning, before the highland mist rolls in.
Stop 2

Sekumpul Waterfall

Widely called the most beautiful waterfall in Bali - a cluster of tall falls dropping into a jungle amphitheatre. It's a proper trek down and back up (about an hour each way, plus a river crossing), so bring shoes you don't mind soaking. Absolutely worth it.
Stop 3

Ulun Danu Beratan Lake Temple

The temple that appears to float on Lake Beratan, wrapped in cool highland air and morning mist. It's on Indonesia's 50,000 rupiah note for a reason - and it's on the way north, so it slots in perfectly.
Stop 4

Munduk Coffee Highlands

A cool mountain village ringed by clove, cacao, and coffee plantations, with valley views running down to the north coast. We stop for a tasting and a proper look at how Balinese coffee actually gets from tree to cup.
Stop 5

Git Git Waterfall

A 35-metre single drop crashing into a pool deep in the rainforest, reached by an easy paved path through coffee and clove trees. Much gentler than Sekumpul, and a good place to cool off without a serious hike.
Stop 6

Banyumala Twin Waterfall

Twin streams falling into a clear turquoise pool, hidden at the bottom of a jungle trek that keeps most of the crowds away. One of the few waterfalls in Bali where you can comfortably swim right at the base.
Stop 7

Lovina Dolphin Sunrise (Optional Add-On)

A traditional outrigger boat leaves the black-sand beach before dawn, and within half an hour you're surrounded by wild spinner dolphins racing the swell. This one requires an overnight stay in the north - ask us and we'll build the itinerary around it.

Banyumala Twin Waterfall

Twin falls dropping into a clear pool you can swim in, at the end of a jungle trek.

About

Banyumala is a twin waterfall in the northern highlands - two streams falling side by side into a wide, clear pool at the base. It takes a short trek down through the jungle to reach, which keeps the crowds away, so you'll often have the place almost to yourself.

It's one of the few Bali waterfalls where you can comfortably swim right at the base, with calm, cool water and greenery all around. Bring a change of clothes - most people can't resist getting in.

Highlights

  • Twin falls into a clear, swimmable pool
  • A short jungle trek keeps the crowds away
  • Lush, green highland setting
  • One of Bali's best waterfalls for swimming

Good to Know

  • Bring a swimsuit, towel & a change of clothes
  • The trek down is steep in places - wear good shoes
  • A small entrance fee usually applies

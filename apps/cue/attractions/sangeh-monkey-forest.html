Sangeh Monkey Forest

A sacred grove of towering nutmeg trees north of Ubud, home to hundreds of grey long-tailed macaques and a moss-covered temple.

The Experience

The Nutmeg Forest

Towering Trees & Temple

Sangeh is a protected grove of giant pala (nutmeg) trees, many over 40 metres tall and centuries old, wrapped around Pura Bukit Sari, a 17th-century temple. Walking the shaded forest path, with light filtering through the canopy, feels a world away from the busy south.

The Monkeys

Cheeky Grey Macaques

The forest is ruled by hundreds of grey long-tailed macaques who are very much at home here. Your guide shows you how to keep belongings safe and interact respectfully - and you can pick up bananas at the entrance to feed them if you like.

Good to Know

An Easy West-Bali Stop

The paths are flat, shaded, and easy going, so it suits all ages - allow around 45 minutes to an hour. Sangeh sits right on the route up toward Bedugul and Tanah Lot, so it slots neatly into a West Bali day.

Highlights

  • A grove of giant nutmeg trees, some over 40 metres tall
  • The 17th-century Pura Bukit Sari temple hidden in the forest
  • Hundreds of resident grey long-tailed macaques
  • Shaded, flat forest paths - easy for all ages

Before You Go

  • Keep sunglasses, hats and loose items packed away from the monkeys
  • Bananas to feed the macaques are sold at the entrance
  • Flat, easy walking on forest paths
  • Morning is cooler and the light through the trees is best

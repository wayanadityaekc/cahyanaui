Lempuyang Temple - Gates of Heaven

Bali's most photographed split gate, framing sacred Mount Agung on a clear morning.

About

Pura Lempuyang Luhur is one of Bali's oldest and highest temples, but it's the split gate (candi bentar) at its entrance - the famous "Gates of Heaven" - that draws visitors from around the world, perfectly framing sacred Mount Agung in the distance. We arrive early to beat the queue, and your guide handles the ticket, sarong, and the well-known mirror-reflection photo so you don't lose half the day waiting in line.

Beyond the viral shot, Lempuyang is a serious place of worship perched high on the mountainside, wrapped in cool air and forest. Climbing to the gate at first light, with Agung emerging from the morning haze, is the kind of moment that feels earned - and far calmer than the midday crowds.

Highlights

  • The iconic "Gates of Heaven" framing Mount Agung
  • One of Bali's six great directional temples
  • Early arrival to skip the worst of the queue
  • Guide handles ticket, sarong & the mirror photo

Good to Know

  • Go early - the photo queue gets very long by mid-morning
  • Sarong required and provided (we arrange it)
  • It's high and cool - bring a light layer; the view needs a clear sky
  • The "reflection" is made with a mirror under the phone, not water

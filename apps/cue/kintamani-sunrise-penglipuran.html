Kintamani Sunrise & Penglipuran Village

Sunrise over an active volcano from a 4x4 jeep, then the traditional village of Penglipuran, the holy springs of Tirta Empul, and the Tegalalang rice terrace on the way home.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Mount Batur Jeep Sunrise

We set off before dawn so a 4x4 jeep can carry you up the black-lava slopes of Mount Batur to a viewpoint for sunrise over the caldera and lake - no strenuous hike required. Coffee in hand, watch the sky turn as the volcano wakes up. The easiest way to catch Bali's most famous sunrise.

Stop 2

Penglipuran Village

One of the cleanest, best-kept traditional villages in Bali - a Bali Aga village with a single spotless main avenue lined with matching gates and courtyard homes. Step into a family compound and see a way of life that has barely changed in centuries. It sits right on the route down from Batur.

Stop 3

Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple

Bali's most sacred spring temple, where Balinese Hindus have performed the melukat purification ritual for over a thousand years. You're welcome to join the ritual yourself - we'll arrange a sarong and walk you through the etiquette so you can take part with respect.

Stop 4

Tegalalang Rice Terrace & Coffee

We finish at the iconic Tegalalang rice terraces, with a stop at a nearby plantation to taste Balinese coffees and the famous kopi luwak. A relaxed, scenic way to close a very early morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car - one price covers up to 5 people, so a fuller car costs less per head. Groups of 6 or more simply take a second car. Only the Exclusive version adds a per-person part: the entrance tickets.

Are entrance tickets included?

Your choice, via the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard keeps tickets out - you pay at each gate, only for the places you enter. Exclusive includes the tickets for the listed stops, so the whole day is prepaid.

How do I book and pay?

Pick your date, tap Book, and confirm on WhatsApp. A 10% deposit secures your car and driver; the balance is paid in cash or by transfer at the end of the tour. Guests staying at our villas skip the deposit entirely.

Do I need to hike?

No - that's the point. A 4x4 jeep carries you up Mount Batur's lava fields to the sunrise viewpoint: the full volcano dawn, zero trekking.

Is it suitable for all ages?

The most all-ages sunrise we offer - kids to grandparents, everyone just rides the jeep. The only challenge is the alarm clock.

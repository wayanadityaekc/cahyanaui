Sekumpul Waterfall

Often called Bali's most beautiful waterfall - a cluster of tall falls in a jungle amphitheatre.

About

Sekumpul is widely called the most beautiful waterfall in Bali - a cluster of tall falls dropping into a lush jungle amphitheatre. Reaching the base is a proper trek down and back up, around an hour each way including a river crossing, so it's an adventure as much as a viewpoint.

The effort is exactly why it stays so wild and uncrowded. Standing at the bottom, surrounded by towering green walls and the roar of several falls at once, is a genuinely unforgettable experience.

Highlights

  • Often rated Bali's most beautiful waterfall
  • A cluster of tall falls in a jungle bowl
  • A real trek - wild and uncrowded
  • Spectacular from the base

Good to Know

  • It's a strenuous hike with a river crossing
  • Wear shoes you don't mind soaking
  • A local trekking guide is required on site

Bali Zoo

Meet elephants, orangutans, sun bears and more at Bali Zoo, a lush jungle wildlife park just south of Ubud - with feeding experiences, animal encounters, and shady garden paths the kids will remember.

The Experience

The Animals

Elephants, Orangutans & Jungle Grounds

Wander shaded jungle paths past elephants, orangutans, sun bears, big cats, and hundreds of tropical animals, all set in green, garden-like grounds. It's compact enough for a relaxed half-day, but rich enough that kids won't want to leave.

Up-Close Experiences

Feeding, Encounters & Night Safari

Beyond the walk-through, there are optional add-ons like animal feeding sessions, an elephant encounter, and breakfast alongside the orangutans. After dark, the park runs a night safari - a completely different way to see it come alive.

Good to Know

A Great Family Half-Day

Paths are shady and mostly easy-going, with cafes and rest spots throughout, so it suits all ages. Allow around two to three hours for the day visit. It's an easy pair with Bali Bird Park nearby for a full wildlife day out from Ubud.

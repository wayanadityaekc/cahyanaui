Coffee Plantation & Tasting

Walk a working Bali coffee and spice plantation and taste a flight of local brews, including the famous kopi luwak.

The Experience

The Plantation

Where Coffee, Cacao & Spices Grow

Wander the shaded grounds of a working Bali plantation, where coffee bushes grow alongside cacao, vanilla, ginger, lemongrass, and other spices. A guide walks you through the garden and points out the plants behind everyday Balinese flavours - it's a relaxed, green stroll that shows you where your cup actually comes from.

Traditional Roasting

Beans Roasted by Hand Over Wood Fire

Watch the beans roasted the old way - stirred by hand in a pan over a wood fire until they darken and release their aroma. It's the same slow method Balinese families have used for generations, and you can smell the difference long before you taste it.

The Tasting Flight

A Row of Local Coffees & Herbal Teas

Settle in for a complimentary tasting flight of local coffees and herbal teas - flavours like ginger coffee, lemongrass tea, and Balinese cocoa lined up to sip and compare. The plantation is also known for kopi luwak, the famous civet coffee, if you'd like to try a cup.

Highlights

  • Stroll a working coffee, cacao, and spice plantation
  • See beans roasted by hand over a wood fire
  • Complimentary tasting flight of local coffees and herbal teas
  • Chance to try the famous kopi luwak (civet coffee)

Before You Go

  • The standard tasting flight is free; a cup of kopi luwak may cost extra
  • Bring a little cash for coffee or spices you'd like to take home
  • Shaded and easy walking - comfortable shoes are enough

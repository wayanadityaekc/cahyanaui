Bali's Beaches & Surf

Where to swim, surf, and catch the sunset around the island.

Bali Guide › What to Do

Bali's coastline changes character as you go around the island. The south has the classic white-sand beaches and reliable surf - Kuta for beginners, Uluwatu for experienced surfers, and Canggu for a bit of both.

The east and north offer quieter black-sand shores and calmer water, better for a peaceful swim than a party. Offshore, coral reefs make for easy snorkeling.

Wherever you swim, respect the ocean - Bali's surf beaches can have strong currents, so watch for flags and lifeguards.

Good to Know

  • South = white sand & surf; east/north = quieter black sand
  • Watch for currents & swim between the flags
  • Reefs offshore for easy snorkeling

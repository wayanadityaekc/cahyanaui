Banjar Hot Spring

Air Panas Banjar is a set of sacred hot springs near Lovina in north Bali, where warm sulphur water pours from carved stone dragon spouts into jungle-fringed bathing pools. It sits well inland from the busy south.

About Banjar Hot Spring

The Springs

Warm Water from Carved Nagas

At Air Panas Banjar, warm sulphur-rich water pours from carved stone dragon (naga) mouths into a series of stone bathing pools. The site has drawn bathers for generations and is held sacred locally, and the water runs comfortably warm rather than scalding. Ease in and let the minerals work on tired muscles.

The Setting

A Jungle Garden Bath

The pools sit in a shaded garden of frangipani and tropical plants, a short, flat walk from the road. It is a calm, photogenic spot, equal parts soak and stroll, and a world away from the crowds of the south. It can fill up in the middle of the day, so mornings tend to be quieter.

Visiting

Between Dolphins and the Trek

It slots neatly between the Lovina sunrise dolphins and the Sekumpul trek: a warm-up after the boat and a loosen-up before the climb. Bring swimwear and a towel, though changing rooms and a small cafe are on site. The sulphur can leave a faint scent on swimwear afterward.

Ubud Culture Day Tour

The artistic soul of Ubud in one day - open with the morning Barong dance, explore Ubud's silver, batik, and painting crafts, visit the carved Batuan temple, wander the royal palace, and close with the mesmerizing Kecak fire dance. Your own private car and a local guide who knows the stories behind each craft.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Barong & Keris Dance

We open the day in Batubulan, where the Barong and Keris dance is performed every morning. The Barong - a lion-like guardian of good - faces the demon queen Rangda in an ancient battle of good against evil, set to a live gamelan orchestra and ending with entranced kris dancers. A dramatic, only-in-Bali way to start.

Stop 2

Ubud Arts & Crafts

Bali's craft traditions in one hands-on stop - the silver workshops of Celuk, a wax-and-dye batik studio, and the intricate Batuan painting style. Watch the artisans, try it yourself, and pick up a keepsake straight from the makers.

Stop 3

Pura Batuan Temple

A beautifully carved 11th-century village temple in Batuan, every gate and shrine covered in intricate paras-stone work. Cool, quiet, and rarely crowded - a calm heritage stop right on the road between the craft villages and Ubud.

Stop 4

Ubud Royal Palace & Art Market

Step inside Puri Saren Agung, the ornate home of Ubud's royal family and the beating heart of the town's arts scene. Just across the road, the traditional market is the place to browse handmade crafts, textiles, and souvenirs - your guide will help you get a fair price.

Stop 5

Kecak Fire Dance

We close the day with the hypnotic Kecak - a circle of dozens of chanting men, no instruments, and a fire-lit retelling of the Ramayana as the sun goes down. Raw, powerful, and an unforgettable end to a full day of Balinese art and culture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes - every departure is 100% private: your own air-conditioned car with your own English-speaking driver & local guide, never shared with other guests. The price is per car for up to 5 people; groups of 6 or more simply take a second car.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

You choose between two versions with the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard covers the private car, petrol, and your crew - entrance tickets are paid at each stop, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. Exclusive adds the entrance and dance performance tickets for everyone in your group, so the whole day is prepaid and you never queue at a ticket window.

How long is the tour, and when does it start?

Plan for up to 8 hours, from the morning Barong to the evening Kecak. Since the car is yours, the start time is up to you - most guests leave Ubud in the morning, and we agree the exact pick-up time when you book.

Can we change or skip stops?

Absolutely - the listed stops are a starting point, not a fixed route. Add a stop, swap one out, or stay longer wherever you're enjoying yourself; just tell us before the day or tell your driver on the road.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Tap the Book button, pick your date, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures your car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

When do the dance performances run?

The morning Barong & Keris dance is performed daily. The evening Kecak fire dance in Ubud runs on Tuesday and Sunday - if your date falls on another day, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the best evening performance for your date.

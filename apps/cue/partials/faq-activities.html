Activities FAQ

Do I need any experience for the adventure activities?

No experience needed for any of them. ATV quads are automatic, rafting is beginner-friendly with a guide in every boat, and the jungle swing runs on a full safety harness. You'll always get a briefing and gear before you start.

Are entrance tickets included?

Yes — for activities and performances, the entrance or activity ticket is included in the price we quote you. Round-trip transport is included too: your driver takes you, waits, and brings you back.

What should I wear and bring?

For ATV and rafting: clothes you don't mind getting muddy or soaked, plus a change and sandals with a strap. For the swing: something photogenic that won't fly up. For volcano sunrise trips: a warm jacket and closed shoes — it's genuinely cold before dawn. Showers and lockers are provided where relevant.

Is there an age or fitness requirement?

It varies by activity — rafting from age 7, ATV solo from 12, and the Mount Batur trek needs reasonable fitness and closed shoes. Cultural performances and the jeep sunrise suit all ages. Tell us who's coming and we'll point you to what fits.

When do the cultural performances start?

The Kecak fire dance runs every Tuesday and Sunday at sunset, and the Barong dance runs daily. Both last about an hour. We handle the tickets and get you there early enough for good seats.

Is transport to the activity included?

Yes. Every activity price includes round-trip transport from your hotel or villa in Ubud — the driver takes you there, waits during the activity, and drives you home. No arranging your own scooter or taxi.

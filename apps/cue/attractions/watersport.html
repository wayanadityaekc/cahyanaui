Watersports at Tanjung Benoa

The Beach

Tanjung Benoa, Bali's Watersport Beach

Tanjung Benoa is a narrow spit on Bali's southern peninsula, just north of Nusa Dua, and the island's main base for watersports. The bay is shallow and sheltered by a reef, which keeps the water calm and suits first-timers as much as thrill-seekers. Operators line the beach, and you choose your activities on the day.

The Rides

Jet Ski, Parasailing, Banana Boat & More

Take the controls of a jet ski, get lifted over the bay on a parasail for a view down the coast, or hang on for a banana boat ride. Many operators also run flyboarding, the underwater seawalker, and snorkelling trips. Each activity is priced separately, so tell your driver what you're after and we'll help you sort it.

Visiting

Guided, and Fine for Beginners

Life jackets are provided and a trained instructor runs each activity, so no experience is needed and most rides suit families. You will get wet, so bring a swimsuit, a towel, and a change of clothes. From Ubud it's a half-day out; your driver takes you down, waits while you're on the water, and drives you straight back.

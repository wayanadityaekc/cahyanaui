Ubud Arts & Crafts

Bali's craft villages around Ubud in one loop - hand-worked silver in Celuk, wax-and-dye batik you can try yourself, and the detailed Batuan painting style. Flat, easy walking, and nothing you have to buy.

About the Craft Villages

Celuk Silver

Celuk, Bali's Silver Village

Celuk has been Bali's centre for gold and silversmithing for generations, and much of the island's jewellery still comes from its family workshops. You can watch artisans hand-shape fine filigree and granulation at the bench, then look through the pieces they've made. Browsing is welcome and buying is entirely optional.

Batik Workshop

Batik, Layer by Layer

Batik is made by drawing hot wax onto cloth with a pen-like canting tool, then dyeing around it so the waxed lines resist the colour, one layer at a time. At a local studio you'll see the process up close and can try the canting yourself to make a piece to take home. Relaxed, hands-on, and good for kids.

Batuan Painting

The Batuan Painting Style

The village of Batuan gave its name to one of Bali's distinctive painting styles: dense, dark, finely detailed scenes of village life and mythology that took shape here in the 1930s. Meet local painters and see how a single canvas, filled almost corner to corner, can take weeks of close brushwork.

Padang Padang Beach

A hidden cove reached through a gap in the rocks, loved by surfers.

About

Padang Padang is a small, beautiful cove you reach by squeezing down a staircase through a gap in the rocks. Made famous by the film Eat Pray Love and long loved by surfers, it's a pocket of white sand hemmed in by cliffs and clear water.

It's compact, so it fills up, but the setting is unforgettable - caves and boulders framing the sand, and a reef break offshore that draws surfers from around the world. A fun, photogenic stop on the south coast.

Highlights

  • A hidden cove through a gap in the rocks
  • Famous surf break just offshore
  • Clear water and dramatic cliffs
  • Great for photos and people-watching

Good to Know

  • The entrance staircase is narrow - watch your step
  • It's small and can get busy - mornings are calmer
  • A small entrance fee applies

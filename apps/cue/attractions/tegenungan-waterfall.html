Tegenungan Waterfall

A powerful jungle waterfall with a natural swimming pool, minutes from Ubud.

About

Tegenungan is a wide, powerful waterfall set in the jungle just south of Ubud, with a natural pool at its base where you can swim. It's one of the few major Bali waterfalls with easy access - a short walk down from the car park, and a genuinely refreshing reward after a morning of temples.

Viewpoints along the top let you take it all in before you head down; at the bottom, the spray, the roar of the water, and the cool pool make it a favourite spot to unwind. Bring a swimsuit if you fancy a dip - it's the kind of place you don't want to rush.

Highlights

  • Wide, powerful waterfall in a lush jungle setting
  • Natural pool at the base you can swim in
  • Easy access - a short walk down from the car park
  • Viewpoints and photo spots along the top

Good to Know

  • Bring a swimsuit & towel if you want to swim
  • Steps down can be slippery - wear good footwear
  • A small entrance fee usually applies

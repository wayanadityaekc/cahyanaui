Breakfast with a Volcano View

The reward at the top of the Batur sunrise trek - a warm breakfast on the caldera rim, looking straight across the volcano and its lake.

The Experience

The View

Breakfast on the Caldera Rim

Once you reach the top, you settle in at a simple viewpoint warung looking straight out over Mount Batur and the crater lake below. With the sun fully up and the clouds still drifting through the valley, it's one of the finest breakfast views in Bali - and a well-earned reward after the climb.

Volcano-Cooked

Eggs Steamed in the Crater's Heat

A local touch: eggs and bananas gently cooked in the natural volcanic steam rising from the crater, served warm with tea or Bali coffee. It's a small, memorable ritual - proof that the mountain under your feet is still very much alive.

Rest & Soak It In

Catch Your Breath

There's no rush here. Take your time over breakfast, grab your photos of the sunrise-lit caldera, and simply enjoy the quiet before the island fully wakes up. When you're ready, the walk back down is easy and mostly downhill.

Good to Know

Highlights

  • Breakfast on the caldera rim overlooking Mount Batur & its lake
  • Eggs & bananas cooked in the natural volcanic steam
  • Warm tea or Bali coffee with a sunrise panorama
  • A relaxed rest after the sunrise trek before heading down

Before You Go

  • It gets cold before sunrise - bring a light jacket
  • Breakfast is included with the trek; bring extra cash for drinks
  • Wear proper shoes for the trek up and down

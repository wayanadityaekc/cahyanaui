Suggested Itineraries

Not sure where to start? Open one of these ready-made plans for inspiration - then copy it into the builder above and tweak it to fit you.

3 Days - Ubud Essentials

Day 1 - Ubud Culture: Monkey Forest, Tegalalang rice terrace, Tirta Empul temple, and Tegenungan waterfall.

Day 2 - Adventure: White-water rafting on the Ayung River in the morning, jungle swing and coffee plantation in the afternoon.

Day 3 - Sunset & Culture: A slow morning, then the Kecak fire dance at sunset to finish the trip.

5 Days - Bali Explorer

Day 1 - Ubud Highlights: Rice terraces, holy water temple, waterfall, and art market.

Day 2 - East Bali: Lempuyang Gates of Heaven, Tirta Gangga water palace, and Sidemen valley.

Day 3 - Volcano Sunrise: Mount Batur jeep sunrise, then a hot spring to warm up.

Day 4 - Adventure Day: ATV ride through the jungle, then the Bali swing.

Day 5 - South Bali: Uluwatu cliff temple, beaches, and Jimbaran seafood dinner.

7 Days - The Complete Bali

Day 1 - Ubud Culture: Temples, rice terraces, and the Monkey Forest.

Day 2 - East Bali: Lempuyang, Besakih mother temple, and Tukad Cepung waterfall.

Day 3 - North Bali: Twin Lakes, Sekumpul waterfall, and Munduk coffee highlands.

Day 4 - West Bali: Ulun Danu lake temple, Jatiluwih rice terraces, and Tanah Lot sunset.

Day 5 - Adventure: Rafting in the morning, cooking class with a local family in the afternoon.

Day 6 - Volcano: Mount Batur sunrise trek, then a relaxed afternoon.

Day 7 - South Bali: GWK, Melasti beach, Uluwatu temple, and the Kecak dance.

Family-Friendly (any length)

Travelling with kids? Lean toward the gentler experiences: the jeep sunrise instead of the trek, the low jungle swing, the cooking class, cultural performances, and short waterfall stops with easy access. Add a beach day in the south. Tell us the ages and we'll pace it right.

How the builder pricing works

As you add tours, activities, and transfers to each day, the total updates live at the bottom. Tours and transfers are priced per car; activities are per person. A 10% deposit confirms your multi-day plan - and guests staying at one of our villas pay nothing upfront.

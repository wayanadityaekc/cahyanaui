Jimbaran Bay Seafood Dinner

Grilled seafood on the sand, with the lights of the bay curving away in the dark.

About

Jimbaran Bay is the classic way to end a day in the south - tables set right on the sand, fresh fish and prawns grilled over coconut husk, and the lights of the bay curving away into the dark. We take you to a warung where the locals eat, not the tourist traps.

With your feet in the sand and the sea a few metres away, it's as much about the setting as the food. A relaxed, memorable dinner to round off the tour.

Highlights

  • Fresh seafood grilled over coconut husk
  • Tables right on the beach at the water's edge
  • A local warung, not a tourist trap
  • A relaxed way to end the day

Good to Know

  • Dinner cost isn't included - pay as you order
  • Bring a light layer for the sea breeze
  • Best enjoyed around sunset and after dark

About Besakih Temple

The Mother Temple

Bali's Holiest Sanctuary

Pura Besakih is the largest and most sacred temple complex on the island, more than 20 separate temples set in terraces up the slope of Mount Agung. Known as the "Mother Temple," it has been the spiritual centre of Balinese Hinduism for over a thousand years. A sarong and a local guide are included, so you visit with the right context and respect.

The Climb

Stairways Past Soaring Shrines

Wide stone stairways climb through the complex, past split gates and rows of tiered meru shrines that stand like dark pagodas against the sky. Each courtyard opens onto another, higher up the slope. Take your time; the scale sinks in as you move up level by level, with the valley falling away below.

The Mountain

Agung Rising Behind

The whole complex faces sacred Mount Agung, Bali's highest and holiest volcano, which rises directly behind the upper shrines. On a clear day the mountain is as memorable as the temple itself, but the weather here shifts fast, so the peak is often wrapped in cloud until it lifts.

Watersport

Bali's southern coast turns up the adrenaline - jet ski across open water, fly high on a parasail, or bounce over the waves on a banana boat. It's the perfect fast-paced half day, and we handle the drive there and back from Ubud.

The Experience

The Beach

Tanjung Benoa - Bali's Watersport Hub

Tanjung Benoa, on Bali's southern peninsula near Nusa Dua, is the island's home of watersports. The bay here is calm and protected, which makes it ideal for everything from gentle rides to high-speed thrills. Operators line the beach, and you pick the activities you want on the day - go big, or keep it easy.

The Rides

Jet Ski, Parasailing, Banana Boat & More

Take the controls of a jet ski, get lifted into the air on a parasail for a bird's-eye view of the coast, or hang on tight for a banana boat ride. Many spots also offer flyboarding, the underwater seawalker, and snorkeling trips. Each activity is priced separately - tell your driver what you're after and we'll help you sort it.

Good to Know

Safe, Guided & Beginner-Friendly

Life jackets are provided and trained instructors run every activity, so no experience is needed - most rides suit first-timers and families. Bring a swimsuit, towel, and a change of clothes; you'll get wet. Your driver waits while you play and drives you straight back to Ubud afterwards.

Snorkeling in East Bali

Calm, clear coastal water with coral gardens and reef fish, a short swim from shore.

About

The calm, clear waters of East Bali offer some of the best easy snorkeling on the island, with coral gardens and colourful reef fish just a short swim from the shore. Gear is provided and it suits complete beginners - a refreshing break in the middle of a day of temples.

After the heat and steps of the temple stops, slipping into cool, glassy water is a welcome change of pace. Float over the reef, watch the fish come and go, and dry off in the sun - a simple, happy pause before the drive back.

Highlights

  • Clear, calm water ideal for easy snorkeling
  • Coral gardens and colourful reef fish near the shore
  • Snorkel gear provided; beginner-friendly
  • A refreshing midday break between temples

Good to Know

  • Bring a swimsuit, towel & a change of clothes
  • Reef-safe sunscreen is best for the coral
  • Conditions depend on weather - your guide picks the best spot

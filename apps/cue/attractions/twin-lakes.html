Twin Lakes Viewpoint (Buyan & Tamblingan)

Two crater lakes side by side in an old volcanic caldera, seen from the ridge.

About

The Twin Lakes - Buyan and Tamblingan - sit side by side in an old volcanic caldera, separated by a thin strip of jungle. From the viewpoint on the ridge above, you can take both in one frame, with forested slopes falling away toward the water.

It's best in the morning, before the highland mist rolls in and softens the view. Cool, quiet, and green, it's a beautiful first stop on the way into the north.

Highlights

  • Two crater lakes in one sweeping view
  • Ridge-top viewpoint over the caldera
  • Cool, forested highland scenery
  • Best light and clarity in the morning

Good to Know

  • Go early - mist builds through the day
  • It's cool up here - bring a light layer
  • A quick, easy roadside viewpoint

Adventure Activities in Bali

ATV, rafting, jungle swings, and volcano treks - all near Ubud.

Bali Guide › What to Do

If you like your holidays active, Bali delivers. Within easy reach of Ubud you can raft white water through jungle gorges, ride an ATV over muddy trails and through cave tunnels, or swing out over a rice-terrace valley.

For an early start, the Mount Batur sunrise trek climbs an active volcano for dawn over the caldera. Most activities suit beginners and include safety gear, briefings, and round-trip transport.

They pair well with a cultural day - adrenaline in the morning, temples or a waterfall in the afternoon.

Ubud Traditional Market

Pasar Ubud, the bustling traditional market in the heart of town.

The Experience

The Morning Market

Produce, Spices & Offerings at Dawn

In the early morning Pasar Ubud is a local produce market - stalls piled with tropical fruit, vegetables, spices, and the flowers Balinese women weave into the daily canang offerings. It's the town waking up, and the best time to see the market as locals actually use it.

Art & Handicrafts

Textiles, Woodcarving & Souvenirs

Later in the day the market fills with stalls of handicrafts - batik and woven textiles, woodcarvings, rattan baskets, beads, and souvenirs of every kind. It's a maze of colour and craft, and a good spot to pick up a keepsake or a gift to take home.

Bargain & Browse

A Lively Slice of Everyday Ubud

Bargaining is part of the fun here - start friendly, take your time, and enjoy the back-and-forth. More than shopping, the market is a lively slice of everyday Balinese life right in central Ubud, with something to catch your eye down every narrow aisle.

Good to Know

AreaCentral Ubud
Best timeEarly morning
Time here~45 min
EntranceFree

Highlights

  • Local produce, spices, and offering flowers in the morning
  • Textiles, woodcarving, baskets, and souvenir stalls by day
  • Bargaining and browsing in the heart of town
  • A lively, authentic slice of everyday Balinese life

Before You Go

  • Bring small cash - most stalls don't take cards
  • Bargain politely; a friendly smile goes a long way
  • Come early to see the produce market before the crowds

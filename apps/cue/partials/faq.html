Frequently Asked Questions

How does payment work?

A 10% deposit confirms your booking, and you settle the rest in cash or by transfer at the end. Guests staying at one of our villas get a special privilege — no upfront payment at all, you simply pay everything afterwards. No card details needed either way.

Are the prices per person or per car?

Tours and route transfers are priced per car (up to 5 passengers) — so the more people you bring, the cheaper it gets per head. Activities and performances are priced per person, since each guest needs their own entrance ticket. Above 5 guests we simply arrange a second car.

Can I change the itinerary during the day?

Absolutely — that's the whole point of a private tour. If you want to stay longer somewhere, skip a stop, or add one, just tell your driver. Stops under an hour cost nothing extra. Nothing is locked in.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

For tours and transfers, entrance tickets are not included — you pay them directly at each site, so you only pay for the places you actually choose to enter. For activities and performances, the entrance ticket is included in the price we quote you.

Where do you pick up from?

We pick up from any hotel or villa in Ubud and the surrounding area at no extra charge. If you're staying further out (Canggu, Seminyak, Sanur, and so on), just tell us where and we'll quote you honestly — no surprise fees on the day.

What happens if it rains?

Bali rain usually passes within an hour, so most tours run as planned — we just reorder the stops to work around it. If the weather makes an activity genuinely unsafe (rafting in a flood, for example), we'll reschedule or cancel it with no charge at all.

Do your drivers speak English?

Yes — every driver speaks conversational English and knows the history and stories behind each stop. They're locals from Ubud, not out-of-town agency drivers, so they know the roads, the timings, and the quiet spots.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Just message us on WhatsApp as early as you can. Deposits are refundable with reasonable notice, and villa guests who paid nothing upfront can cancel free of charge — we only ask that you let us know so we can free up the driver.

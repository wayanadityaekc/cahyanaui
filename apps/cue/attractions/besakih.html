Besakih - The Mother Temple

Bali's largest and holiest temple, built in terraces up the slopes of Mount Agung.

About

Pura Besakih is Bali's largest and holiest temple complex - more than 20 temples set in terraces up the slope of sacred Mount Agung. Known as the "Mother Temple," it has been the spiritual heart of the island for over a thousand years. Sarong and guide are included so you can explore it with the right context and respect.

Climbing the wide stone stairways past soaring shrines and split gates, with Agung rising behind and the valley falling away below, gives a real sense of why this place is so revered. On a clear day, the mountain views are as memorable as the temple itself.

Highlights

  • Bali's largest and most sacred temple complex
  • Over 20 temples on the slopes of Mount Agung
  • Dramatic tiered shrines, gates & mountain views
  • Sarong and guide included

Good to Know

  • Modest dress required - sarong & sash (we arrange it)
  • Some climbing and steps - comfortable shoes help
  • Mountain weather can change - bring a light layer
  • A local temple guide fee may apply at the entrance

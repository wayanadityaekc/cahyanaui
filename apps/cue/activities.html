Bali Activities & Cultural Performances

From jungle ATV rides and white-water rafting to the mesmerizing Kecak fire dance, discover authentic Balinese adventures and performances in and around Ubud - all with local guides and honest prices.

Must See

Kecak Dance

Bali's iconic fire-and-chant ritual, performed by a circle of over fifty voices, hypnotic spectacle that stays with you long after the flames die down. Performed every Tuesday & Sunday evening.

Handara Gate

The towering Balinese gate framed by jungle-covered mountains.

About

Handara Gate is one of Bali's most recognisable photo spots - a towering traditional split gate framed by jungle-covered mountains, standing at the entrance of a highland golf resort. There's no temple behind it; the draw is purely the dramatic mountain backdrop.

It's a quick stop, and timing is everything - we aim for a window when the queue is short so you get your shot without a long wait. On a clear highland morning, the green peaks behind the gate make it well worth the pause.

Highlights

  • One of Bali's most iconic gate photos
  • Dramatic jungle-mountain backdrop
  • Set in the cool northern highlands
  • We time it to dodge the worst of the queue

Good to Know

  • It's a photo stop - expect a short queue
  • A small fee applies for the photo spot
  • Cool highland air - bring a light layer

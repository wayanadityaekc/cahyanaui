Ulun Danu Beratan & Tanah Lot Temple

A cool highland loop up to the temple that floats on Lake Beratan, the famous Handara gate, and the UNESCO Jatiluwih rice terraces - ending with the sea breaking around Tanah Lot as the sky turns gold.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Ulun Danu Beratan Lake Temple

The temple that seems to float on Lake Beratan - the one printed on Indonesia's 50,000 rupiah note. Up in the highlands the air is cool and the mist rolls in off the water, which makes for the kind of photo you can't get anywhere else on the island.

Stop 2

Handara Gate

The towering Balinese gate framed by jungle-covered mountains - one of the island's most recognisable photo spots. It sits at the entrance of a highland golf resort, and we time it so you get your shot without the long queue.

Stop 3

Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

A UNESCO World Heritage landscape and, honestly, more impressive than Tegalalang - endless green terraces stretching to the horizon with almost no crowds. Still farmed the traditional subak way, exactly as it has been for a thousand years.

Stop 4

Tanah Lot Sunset Temple

The grand finale - a sea temple perched on a rock offshore, cut off by the tide as the sun drops behind it. We arrive with enough time to walk the cliff path and find a good spot before the light turns. Easily one of the best sunsets in Southeast Asia.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car - one price covers up to 5 people, so a fuller car costs less per head. Groups of 6 or more simply take a second car. Only the Exclusive version adds a per-person part: the entrance tickets.

Are entrance tickets included?

Your choice, via the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard keeps tickets out - you pay at each gate, only for the places you enter. Exclusive includes the tickets for the listed stops, so the whole day is prepaid.

How do I book and pay?

Pick your date, tap Book, and confirm on WhatsApp. A 10% deposit secures your car and driver; the balance is paid in cash or by transfer at the end of the tour. Guests staying at our villas skip the deposit entirely.

What time do we get back?

The day ends with sunset at Tanah Lot (around 6-6:30 PM year-round), so you're back in Ubud in the evening. Plan dinner accordingly - or let your driver suggest a stop on the way home.

What if it's cloudy for the sunset?

No one can promise a sunset - that's nature. Tanah Lot is dramatic even under clouds, and the route is flexible enough to work around the weather.

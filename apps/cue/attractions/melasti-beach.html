Melasti Beach

Turquoise water and white sand at the foot of a road carved through a limestone cliff.

About

Melasti is one of the cleanest beaches in the south, and you reach it by driving down a road carved straight through a towering limestone cliff - the approach alone is worth the trip. At the bottom you'll find turquoise water, white sand, and dramatic white walls rising on either side.

It's a favourite for a swim and a wander, with calm water and space to relax. The cliffs make it feel tucked away from the world, even when the south is busy.

Highlights

  • Dramatic cliff-cut road down to the beach
  • Turquoise water and clean white sand
  • Towering limestone walls on either side
  • Great for a swim and photos

Good to Know

  • Bring a swimsuit & towel for a dip
  • Little natural shade - bring sun protection
  • A small entrance/parking fee usually applies

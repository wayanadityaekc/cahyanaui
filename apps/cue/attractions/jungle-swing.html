Bali Jungle Swing

The jungle swing is a modern Bali attraction: a harnessed seat that arcs out over a river valley near Ubud. It's built for photos as much as thrills, and the harness makes it less alarming than it looks.

Riding the Jungle Swing

The Swing

Out Over the Valley

Bali's jungle swings appeared around Ubud in the 2010s and spread quickly. You're strapped into a harness, seated on a wooden swing anchored to a tree at the edge of the gorge, and then pushed. For a few seconds there is genuinely nothing beneath you but jungle canopy and the river far below. Each ride lasts about a minute, and people usually want to go straight back on.

The Heights

From Gentle to Genuinely Wild

Most sites offer several swings at different heights - a low, gentle one for a first go, and a high one that flings you well out over the valley. There are usually bird's-nest platforms, hanging beds, and flower pods too, all set up for photos. Take the low swing first; you'll want the high one afterwards.

Before You Ride

Safety, Photos & What to Wear

Every rider wears a full safety harness clipped to a separate line, so the swing is secure and staff are trained. Wear something that photographs well and won't fly up (long dresses are popular for a reason, but bring shorts underneath). Staff take photos on your phone for free and go slow if you ask, and transport from Ubud is included.

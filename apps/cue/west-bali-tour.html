West Bali Tour

Royal temples, a sacred monkey forest, cool mountain lakes, and UNESCO rice terraces - a west-Bali loop that ends with the sea breaking around Tanah Lot as the sky turns gold.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Taman Ayun Royal Temple

The garden temple of the old Mengwi kingdom, surrounded by a wide moat and rows of tiered meru shrines. A UNESCO site and one of the most elegant, uncrowded temples on the island - a calm first stop on the way to the highlands.

Stop 2

Sangeh Monkey Forest

A sacred grove of giant nutmeg trees wrapped around a 17th-century temple, and home to hundreds of cheeky grey macaques. Shaded, flat paths make it an easy, atmospheric stop - your guide shows you how to keep your things safe from the monkeys.

Stop 3

Ulun Danu Beratan Lake Temple

The temple that seems to float on Lake Beratan - the one printed on Indonesia's 50,000 rupiah note. Up in the highlands the air is cool and the mist rolls in off the water, which makes for the kind of photo you can't get anywhere else on the island.

Stop 4

Handara Gate

The towering Balinese gate framed by jungle-covered mountains - one of the island's most recognisable photo spots. It sits at the entrance of a highland golf resort, and we time it so you get your shot without the long queue.

Stop 5

Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

A UNESCO World Heritage landscape and, honestly, more impressive than Tegalalang - endless green terraces stretching to the horizon with almost no crowds. Still farmed the traditional subak way, exactly as it has been for a thousand years.

Stop 6

Tanah Lot Sunset Temple

The grand finale - a sea temple perched on a rock offshore, cut off by the tide as the sun drops behind it. We arrive with enough time to walk the cliff path and find a good spot before the light turns. Easily one of the best sunsets in Southeast Asia.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes - every departure is 100% private: your own air-conditioned car with your own English-speaking driver & local guide, never shared with other guests. The price is per car for up to 5 people; groups of 6 or more simply take a second car.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

You choose between two versions with the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard covers the private car, petrol, and your crew - entrance tickets are paid at each stop, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. Exclusive adds entrance tickets for all the listed attractions for everyone in your group, so the whole day is prepaid and you never queue at a ticket window.

How long is the tour, and when does it start?

Plan for up to 9 hours, timed so you reach Tanah Lot for sunset. Since the car is yours, the start time is up to you - most guests leave Ubud in the morning, and we agree the exact pick-up time when you book.

Can we change or skip stops?

Absolutely - the listed stops are a starting point, not a fixed route. Add a stop, swap one out, or stay longer wherever you're enjoying yourself; just tell us before the day or tell your driver on the road.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Tap the Book button, pick your date, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures your car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

Do I need a sarong for the temples?

No need to pack one - sarong rental at the temples is already included on this tour. Dress modestly (shoulders covered) and you're set for Taman Ayun, Ulun Danu Beratan, and Tanah Lot.

Ubud Culture Day

The artistic soul of Ubud in one day - open with the morning Barong dance, watch master silversmiths at work in Celuk, try your own hand at batik, wander the royal palace, and close with the mesmerizing Kecak fire dance. Your own private car and a local guide who knows the stories behind each craft.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Barong & Keris Dance

We open the day in Batubulan, where the Barong and Keris dance is performed every morning. The Barong - a lion-like guardian of good - faces the demon queen Rangda in an ancient battle of good against evil, set to a live gamelan orchestra and ending with entranced kris dancers. A dramatic, only-in-Bali way to start.

Stop 2

Celuk - The Silver Village

Just south of Ubud, Celuk has been Bali's home of gold and silversmithing for generations. Watch artisans hand-shape delicate filigree and granulation the same way their grandparents did, then browse the workshops for a one-of-a-kind piece straight from the people who made it.

Stop 3

Batik Workshop

Batik is one of Indonesia's most treasured art forms. At a local studio you'll see how hot wax and natural dyes build up a pattern layer by layer - then pick up the canting tool and make your own piece to take home. Hands-on, relaxed, and a genuine keepsake.

Stop 4

Ubud Royal Palace & Art Market

Step inside Puri Saren Agung, the ornate home of Ubud's royal family and the beating heart of the town's arts scene. Just across the road, the traditional market is the place to browse handmade crafts, textiles, and souvenirs - your guide will help you get a fair price.

Stop 5

Kecak Fire Dance

We close the day with the hypnotic Kecak - a circle of dozens of chanting men, no instruments, and a fire-lit retelling of the Ramayana as the sun goes down. Raw, powerful, and an unforgettable end to a full day of Balinese art and culture.

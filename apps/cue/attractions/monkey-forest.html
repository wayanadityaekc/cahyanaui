Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary

A moss-covered jungle temple in the heart of Ubud, home to hundreds of macaques.

About

The Sacred Monkey Forest is a jungle temple complex right in the middle of Ubud, home to more than 700 long-tailed macaques. Ancient banyan roots, moss-covered stone guardians, and three holy temples make it one of Bali's most atmospheric sanctuaries - a cool, green world just steps from the busy town centre.

It's a nature reserve and spiritual site as much as a monkey attraction. The macaques roam freely and are used to visitors, so your guide will show you how to keep your belongings safe and interact respectfully. Walking the shaded paths beneath towering trees is a peaceful way to end a day of exploring.

Highlights

  • Home to 700+ free-roaming long-tailed macaques
  • Ancient jungle temples and moss-covered stone carvings
  • Towering banyan trees and shaded forest paths
  • Right in central Ubud - easy to reach

Good to Know

  • Don't bring loose food, drinks, or dangling items - monkeys are curious
  • Don't touch or feed the monkeys; follow your guide's tips
  • A small entrance fee applies

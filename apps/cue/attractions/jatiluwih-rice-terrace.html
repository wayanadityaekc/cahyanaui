Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

A UNESCO World Heritage sea of green terraces, farmed the traditional subak way.

About

Jatiluwih is a UNESCO World Heritage landscape of rice terraces stretching to the horizon, and many travellers find it even more impressive than Tegalalang - far larger, far quieter, and still farmed the traditional subak way exactly as it has been for a thousand years.

Walking trails wind through the paddies, so you can actually step into the landscape rather than just view it from the road. The scale, the silence, and the layered greens make it one of Bali's most peaceful stops.

Highlights

  • Vast UNESCO World Heritage rice terraces
  • Far quieter than Bali's busier terrace spots
  • Walking trails through the working paddies
  • Traditional subak irrigation still in use

Good to Know

  • Wear comfortable shoes for the trails
  • Cooler highland climate - a light layer helps
  • A small entrance/eco fee usually applies

Batur Sunrise & Adrenaline

Summit an active volcano for sunrise above the clouds, then trade the trail for a jungle ATV ride through mud, rivers, and rice fields - the ultimate one-day adventure out of Ubud.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

A pre-dawn start and a guided climb up the 1,717-metre active volcano, reaching the summit just as the sun breaks over Lake Batur and Mount Agung. The trail is moderate and no technical experience is needed - just a head torch, warm layer, and a sense of adventure.

Stop 2

Breakfast with a Volcano View

At the top, breakfast is served with one of Bali's best views - including eggs gently cooked in the volcanic steam rising from the crater. Catch your breath, take it all in, then head back down as the island wakes up.

Stop 3

Jungle ATV Quad Ride

Swap boots for a quad bike and tear through jungle tracks, splash across rivers, roll past rice fields, and blast through a cave tunnel. Full safety gear and a briefing included - drive solo or ride tandem. Messy, loud, and seriously fun.

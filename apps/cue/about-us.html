About Us

We're a local team from Ubud who knows the island beyond the postcards — and we'd rather show you the real Bali than the one on the brochure.

Our Story

Who We Are

Born and Raised in Ubud

Cahyana Ubud Experience started with one car, one driver, and a simple idea: that visitors deserve to see Bali the way locals actually live it. We're not an agency with a call centre. We're a small family team from Ubud — the same people who answer your message are the ones who show up at your hotel in the morning.

How We Work

Your Trip, Your Pace

Nothing about our tours is locked in. Want to stay longer at a waterfall, skip a temple, or stop for coffee at a warung you spotted from the car? Just say so. Our drivers know the roads, the timings, and the quiet hours at every stop — so you get the view without the queue, and the day bends around you instead of the other way round.

What We Believe

Honest Prices, No Games

The price you're quoted is the price you pay. No commission stops at overpriced souvenir shops, no surprise fees on the day, no pressure to buy anything. Just a small 10% deposit to confirm your booking, and you settle the rest at the end — and if you're staying at one of our villas, you pay nothing upfront at all. If that sounds fair, you haven't booked many tours in Bali.

Why travel with us

Because the difference between a good day in Bali and a forgettable one is almost never the destination — it's who takes you there, how well they know it, and whether they're honest with you about the rest.

Pandawa Beach

A once-secret beach reached through a dramatic cut in the cliff lined with carved statues.

The Experience

The Approach

A Cliff Cut Lined with Statues

Pandawa was once called "Secret Beach" because it hid behind a limestone ridge. Today you reach it through a dramatic cut carved into the cliff, lined on both sides with giant statues of the Pandawa heroes set into the rock. It's a grand, memorable arrival that builds anticipation before the sea even comes into view.

The Beach

Calm Water & Wide Open Sand

The cut opens onto calm water, wide sand, and space to actually breathe - far more relaxed than the busier beaches of the south. It's a good swim stop, with gentle water that's easy for a dip. There's room to spread out and settle in for a while under the cliffs.

The Water

Loungers, Kayaks & Gentle Swimming

The gentle conditions make Pandawa great for swimming and paddling, and you can rent loungers and kayaks right on the sand. It's an easy, laid-back stretch of coast for a relaxed few hours by the water. Bring a swimsuit and towel to make the most of it.

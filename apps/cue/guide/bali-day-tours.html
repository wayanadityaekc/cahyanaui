Guided Day Tours in Bali

See the highlights with a local driver-guide - no stress, no driving.

Bali Guide › What to Do

With so much spread across the island, a private day tour is the easiest way to see a lot without the stress of driving or navigating Bali's traffic. A local driver-guide handles the route, the logistics, and the context behind each stop.

We run themed day tours across the island - Ubud for culture and nature, East and West Bali for temples and highlands, South Bali for beaches and clifftops, and North Bali for waterfalls and lakes.

Each is private, fully customisable, and priced upfront, with hotel or villa pick-up in Ubud.

How Our Tours Work

Each tour is a private car with a local driver-guide, not a group coach, so the day flexes around you - linger longer at a spot you love, skip one that doesn't appeal, or add a stop on the way. Pick-up and drop-off are at your hotel or villa in the Ubud area.

We share the route and the price upfront, with no pressure to stop at commission-based shops. If you can't decide between themes, we're happy to suggest an itinerary based on how long you're staying and what you most want to see.

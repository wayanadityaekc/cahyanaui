Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking

Mount Batur is an active volcano of 1,717 metres in the Kintamani caldera, northeast of Ubud. Its pre-dawn hike takes about two hours up volcanic rock to the rim for sunrise over Lake Batur, and pick-up is around 2 AM.

Climbing Mount Batur

The Climb

Two Hours Up, in the Dark

Pick-up is around 2:00 AM, and you start hiking by torchlight at about 4:00. The trail rises to 1,717 metres through volcanic sand and loose rock - steep in places, but no climbing skill needed. It takes most people about two hours. Your guide sets a pace the whole group can hold, and there's no rush.

The Summit

Sunrise Above the Clouds

You reach the rim just before dawn, and the sky lights up behind Mount Abang and Mount Agung while cloud sits in the caldera below you. Lake Batur appears out of the dark. It's cold and you'll be tired, and for the first few minutes almost nobody talks - it's that kind of view.

Before You Climb

Fitness, Warmth & Volcanic Breakfast

You need reasonable fitness and closed hiking shoes - this is a real hike, not a stroll. Bring a warm jacket; the summit sits around 10°C before sunrise. At the top your guide cooks eggs and banana sandwiches in the steam vents of the volcano itself, which never stops being a good story to tell.

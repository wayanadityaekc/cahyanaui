Tegal Wangi Beach

A small cove below the cliffs near Jimbaran, reached by a steep path that keeps it quiet. Shallow pools form in the volcanic rock at low tide, and the west-facing bay catches the late-afternoon sun.

About Tegal Wangi Beach

The Cove

A Steep Path to a Quiet Beach

Tegal Wangi sits at the foot of the limestone cliffs a short drive from Jimbaran, reached by a steep path and some rough steps down from the parking area. The climb keeps numbers down, so even on a busy day the cove stays fairly quiet. Wear shoes with grip; the path is loose and can be slippery after rain.

The Rock Pools

Natural Pools in the Volcanic Rock

At low tide, shallow pools fill in the dark volcanic rock along the shore, the "natural jacuzzi" people come for, where you can sit in calm water with the waves breaking just beyond. They only appear when the tide is out, so timing matters. When the tide is in, the pools disappear and the water reaches the base of the cliff.

Sunset and the Cliffs

West-Facing Light Over the Bay

The bay faces west between limestone headlands, which makes it a favourite for late-afternoon light and sunset. It is more a place to sit, wade, and take it in than a swimming beach, since the surf can push hard against the rocks. There is almost no natural shade, so bring sun cover and check the tide times before you go.

Celuk - The Silver Village

Just south of Ubud, Celuk has crafted gold and silver for generations. Watch the artisans at work, then find a handmade piece that's truly one of a kind - straight from the people who made it.

The Experience

The Craft

Filigree, by Hand

Celuk's silversmiths are known for filigree and granulation - fine threads and tiny beads of silver worked into intricate patterns. In the family workshops you can watch each piece take shape by hand, a skill passed down through generations.

Take One Home

Straight From the Maker

Browse rings, pendants, and earrings in the workshop galleries - buying directly from the people who made them, usually at fairer prices than the tourist shops. Your guide can help you tell hand-made from mass-produced.

Optional

Make Your Own Ring

Some workshops offer short silversmithing classes where you shape and finish your own ring to take home - a hands-on souvenir with a real story behind it. Ask your guide if you'd like to add it. [ISI WAYAN: konfirmasi ketersediaan & harga workshop]

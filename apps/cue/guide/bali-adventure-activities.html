ATV, rafting, jungle swings, and volcano treks - all near Ubud.

Bali's interior is an adventure park that happens to also be a landscape: rivers with real rapids, jungle trails made for quad bikes, valleys deep enough to swing over, and a volcano you can summit before breakfast. Almost all of it sits within an hour of Ubud, needs no experience, and comes with gear, briefing, and transport included. Here's the line-up and who each one suits.

The Line-Up

ATV quad biking. Automatic quads through jungle tracks, rice fields, river crossings, and a cave tunnel. No licence needed, riding solo from age 12, and the mud is the feature, not the bug - showers and lockers wait at the base. The anchor of our ATV Adventure day.

White-water rafting. Twelve kilometres of class II-III rapids down the Ayung river gorge - lively enough to soak everyone, tame enough for first-timers and kids from age 7, with a professional guide steering every raft. Built into our Rafting Adventure day.

The jungle swing. Harnessed swings arcing out over a river valley, with photo nests and several heights. Two minutes of flying, an hour of photos, zero fitness required.

Batur sunrise trek / jeep. The big early one - two hours of dark climbing to a volcano summit, or the 4x4 shortcut to the same dawn. Details in the volcano guide.

Watersports at Tanjung Benoa. Jet ski, parasailing, banana boat - the south-coast adrenaline strip, usually taken as part of the South Bali day.

Who Can Do What

  • Kids - rafting from 7, ATV solo from 12 (younger tandem: message us the ages), swing & jeep sunrise suit all ages
  • Non-swimmers - rafting is fine: life jackets & helmets are mandatory, guides do the steering
  • Grandparents - jeep sunrise & the swing over the trek & the quads
  • Pregnant travellers - operators advise against ATV, rafting & the trek; the day's other stops still work
  • The nervous - every activity starts with a briefing, and "slower please" is always an option

What to Wear & Bring

Clothes you're happy to sacrifice to mud or river, a full change for afterwards, sandals with a strap or old trainers, and a dry bag for the phone. Rafting and ATV bases have showers and lockers, so you can rejoin civilisation presentable. For the sunrise trips, swap all of that for a warm jacket and closed shoes - the volcano before dawn is a different climate.

Rain Changes Less Than You'd Think

Adventure days are the most weather-proof part of a Bali trip. Rafting in drizzle is arguably better than in sunshine - you're soaked by design, and the river runs livelier. ATV trails turn from dusty to gloriously muddy, which is what most riders secretly wanted. The swing is the exception: it pauses for rain and lightning, and the photos want blue sky anyway, so it slots best into a dry morning. Only sustained heavy rain reshuffles the day - and when it does, the operator makes the call and we rebuild the schedule around it, usually within the same day.

About Those Swing Photos

The jungle swing is two parts ride, eight parts photography, so play it as intended: wear something that moves (the flowy-dress-in-the-wind picture exists because it works), go in the morning for soft light and shorter queues, and hand your phone to the staff - they shoot dozens of these a day and know every angle. The photo nests and flower thrones beside the swings are included at some sites and a small extra at others; decide by mood, not obligation.

Safety, Honestly

These are commercial activities run daily by licensed operators with proper gear - briefings before, guides throughout, and equipment that gets used hard and replaced. Two honest notes anyway: check that your travel insurance actually covers adventure activities (many base policies exclude them, and the upgrade is cheap), and accept that weather calls belong to the operators - if the river is too high to raft safely, the answer is no, and we rebuild the day around it. That "no" is the system working.

Building It into Your Trip

The efficient pattern is adventure-plus: adrenaline in the morning while energy is high, something gentle after - a waterfall, a temple, a long lunch. That's exactly how our combo days are shaped: ATV + Bali Zoo & Bird Park, rafting + rice terraces & coffee, and the Batur trek + hot springs. One booking, one car, and the logistics stop being your problem.

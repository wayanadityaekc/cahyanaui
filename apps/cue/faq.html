Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book?

Pick a tour, activity, or transfer, choose your date, and send us a message on WhatsApp - or use the booking form on any page. We reply fast, confirm your driver and time, and that's it. No account, no long forms.

How does payment work?

A 10% deposit confirms your booking - you pay the remaining balance in cash or by transfer at the end of your tour. Guests staying at one of our villas enjoy a special privilege: no upfront payment at all, you simply settle everything afterwards.

Can I build my own multi-day itinerary?

Yes - that's exactly what our My Trips planner is for. Add tours, activities, performances, and transfers, pick a date for each, and watch the total update as you plan. Prefer us to plan it for you? Just tell us what you like and we'll shape a route around it.

Are prices per person or per car?

Tours and route transfers are priced per car (up to 5 passengers), so bringing more people lowers the cost per head. Activities and performances are per person, since each guest needs their own entrance ticket.

Where do you pick up from?

Free pick-up from any hotel or villa in Ubud and the surrounding area. Staying further out - Canggu, Seminyak, Sanur? Tell us where and we'll quote you honestly, with no surprise fees on the day.

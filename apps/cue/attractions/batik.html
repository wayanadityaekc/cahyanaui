Batik Workshop

Batik is one of Indonesia's most treasured art forms. At a local studio you'll see how wax and dye build a pattern layer by layer - then make your own piece to take home. Hands-on, relaxed, and a genuine keepsake.

The Experience

The Art

Wax, Dye & Patience

Batik is made by drawing with hot wax, then dyeing the cloth so the waxed areas resist the colour - repeated layer by layer to build up a pattern. You'll see the canting tool and copper stamps in action, and learn how a single piece can take days.

Hands-On

Make Your Own

With an artisan guiding you, pick up the canting and apply the wax yourself, then choose your colours and dye the cloth. No experience needed - it's forgiving, meditative, and genuinely fun, whatever your artistic skill.

Take It Home

Your Own Piece

Once it's dried, your finished batik is yours to keep - a wearable or framable souvenir you actually made. Far more personal than anything off a shop shelf. [ISI WAYAN: konfirmasi durasi, harga & lokasi studio]

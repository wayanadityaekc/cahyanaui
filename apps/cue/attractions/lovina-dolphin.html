Lovina Dolphin Sunrise (Optional Add-On)

A dawn boat trip from Lovina's black-sand coast to see wild spinner dolphins.

About

At Lovina on the north coast, a traditional outrigger boat leaves the black-sand beach before dawn, and within half an hour you're often surrounded by wild spinner dolphins racing the swell. It's a magical, only-in-the-north experience as the sun comes up over the calm Bali Sea.

Because it starts so early and lies far from Ubud, this one works best with an overnight stay in the north - just ask and we'll build the itinerary around it.

Highlights

  • Sunrise boat trip to see wild spinner dolphins
  • Traditional outrigger from a black-sand beach
  • Calm northern sea and a beautiful dawn
  • A unique north-coast experience

Good to Know

  • Requires an overnight stay in the north
  • Very early start - dress warm for the dawn
  • Dolphins are wild - sightings are common, not guaranteed

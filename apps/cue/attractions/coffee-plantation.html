Inside a Bali Coffee Plantation

The Plantation

Where Coffee, Cacao & Spices Grow

Wander the shaded grounds of a working Bali plantation, where coffee bushes grow alongside cacao, vanilla, ginger, lemongrass, and other spices. A guide walks you through the garden and points out the plants behind everyday Balinese flavours, so you see where your cup actually starts. It's an easy, level stroll.

Traditional Roasting

Beans Roasted by Hand Over Wood Fire

Watch the beans roasted the old way, stirred by hand in a pan over a wood fire until they darken and release their aroma. It's the same slow method Balinese families have used for generations, and you can smell the difference well before you taste it.

The Tasting Flight

A Row of Local Coffees & Herbal Teas

Settle in for a complimentary tasting flight of local coffees and herbal teas, flavours like ginger coffee, lemongrass tea, and Balinese cocoa lined up to sip and compare. The plantation is also known for kopi luwak, the civet coffee, which costs extra if you'd like to try a cup.

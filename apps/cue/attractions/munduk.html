Munduk Coffee Highlands

Munduk sits high in Bali's northern hills, in coffee country ringed by clove and cacao plantations. The air is cool and the roads up are winding, but the valley views run down toward the north coast.

About Munduk

The Village

Highland Coffee Country

Munduk lies high in Bali's northern mountains, above the twin crater lakes of Buyan and Tamblingan. The Dutch planted coffee, clove, and cacao across these hills in the colonial era, and the crops still shape both the landscape and the local economy. Cool air, quiet lanes, and valley views running down to the north coast make it feel a long way from the beach resorts of the south.

The Plantations

Coffee, Clove & Cacao at the Source

The village is ringed by working coffee, clove, and cacao plantations, and a tasting stop shows how Balinese coffee gets from tree to cup. You can walk among the trees, see beans and pods growing on the branch, and sample a range of local brews and herbal teas. Tasting costs are usually small and pay-as-you-go, so there is no pressure to buy.

Visiting

Walks, Waterfalls & Mountain Air

Beyond the tastings, Munduk is laced with walking trails and waterfalls tucked into the folds of the hills, and it draws travellers who prefer their Bali green and quiet. The roads up are winding, so a stop here is also a chance to stretch and breathe the cool mountain air. Bring a light layer, since the highlands stay noticeably cooler than the coast.

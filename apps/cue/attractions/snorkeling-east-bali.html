Snorkeling in East Bali

Calm, clear coastal water with coral gardens and reef fish, a short swim from shore.

The Experience

The Water

Calm, Clear East-Coast Sea

The east coast around Amed and the Blue Lagoon is sheltered and calm, with water clear enough to see the reef from the surface. You wade in straight from the beach - no boat needed - and within a few strokes you're floating over the coral. It's the kind of easy, glassy water that makes snorkeling relaxing rather than daunting.

The Reef

Coral Gardens & Reef Fish

Just a short swim from shore, the reef comes alive with coral and schools of colourful fish. Float quietly and they drift right up around you, weaving through the coral heads. It's a gentle, absorbing window into Bali's underwater life - and a cool change of pace after the temples and steps of the day.

Easy for Beginners

Gear Provided, No Experience Needed

Mask, snorkel, and fins are provided, and the calm shallows suit complete first-timers. Your guide reads the conditions on the day and picks the sheltered spot with the best visibility. Bring a swimsuit and towel, float at your own pace, and dry off in the sun before the drive back.

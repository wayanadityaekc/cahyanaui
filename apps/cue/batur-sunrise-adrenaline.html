Mount Batur Sunrise Trek & Hot Springs

Summit an active volcano for sunrise above the clouds, refuel with a volcano-view breakfast, then ease tired legs in natural hot springs by Lake Batur - the ultimate one-day adventure out of Ubud.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

A pre-dawn start and a guided climb up the 1,717-metre active volcano, reaching the summit just as the sun breaks over Lake Batur and Mount Agung. The trail is moderate and no technical experience is needed - just a head torch, warm layer, and a sense of adventure.

Stop 2

Breakfast with a Volcano View

At the top, breakfast is served with one of Bali's best views - including eggs gently cooked in the volcanic steam rising from the crater. Catch your breath, take it all in, then head back down as the island wakes up.

Stop 3

Natural Hot Spring by Lake Batur

We finish with a soak in natural volcanic hot springs on the shore of Lake Batur. Mineral water warmed underground by the volcano eases tired legs after the sunrise trek, with the caldera and lake around you. The perfect, relaxing end to a big morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes - every departure is 100% private: your own air-conditioned car with your own driver, plus a licensed trekking guide on the mountain, never shared with other guests. The price is per car for up to 5 people; groups of 6 or more simply take a second car.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

You choose between two versions with the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard covers the private car, petrol, and your crew - entrance tickets are paid at each stop, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. Exclusive adds the trekking, breakfast, and hot spring fees for everyone in your group, so the whole day is prepaid and you never queue at a ticket window.

How long is the tour, and when does it start?

Plan for up to 10 hours. This is a pre-dawn adventure - pick-up from Ubud is very early so you reach the summit before first light; we confirm the exact time when you book.

Can we change or skip stops?

Absolutely - the listed stops are a starting point, not a fixed route. Add a stop, swap one out, or stay longer wherever you're enjoying yourself; just tell us before the day or tell your driver on the road.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Tap the Book button, pick your date, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures your car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

How hard is the sunrise trek?

It's roughly a two-hour pre-dawn hike to the 1,717 m summit - steady and steep in places, but done at your pace with a licensed local guide and headlamps. Wear proper shoes and bring a jacket: it's genuinely cold at the top until the sun comes up. Breakfast at the summit and a hot spring soak afterwards are your reward.

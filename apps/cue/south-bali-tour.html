South Bali Tour

A day on Bali's southern peninsula - watersports at Tanjung Benoa, the giant Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue, then the white sands of Pandawa and Melasti. Beaches, big views, a splash of adventure.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Watersports at Tanjung Benoa

Start the day out on the water at Tanjung Benoa, Bali's watersport hub. Pick from jet ski, banana boat, parasailing, or a glass-bottom boat over to Turtle Island. Gear and instructors are on hand, so first-timers are welcome. A fun, splashy way to kick things off.
Stop 2

Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK)

One of the tallest statues on earth - Lord Vishnu riding the mythical Garuda, standing 121 metres above a limestone cultural park. Taller than the Statue of Liberty, and it took 28 years to finish. Impressive up close in a way photos never quite capture.
Stop 3

Pandawa Beach

Once called "Secret Beach" because it was hidden behind a limestone ridge, now reached through a dramatic cut in the cliff lined with giant carved statues. Calm water, wide sand, and space to actually breathe. A good swim stop before lunch.
Stop 4

Melasti Beach

You reach it by driving down a road carved straight through a limestone cliff - the approach alone is worth it. At the bottom: turquoise water, white sand, and towering white walls on either side. One of the cleanest beaches in the south, and a relaxed way to end the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car - one price covers up to 5 people, so a fuller car costs less per head. Groups of 6 or more simply take a second car. Only the Exclusive version adds a per-person part: the entrance tickets.

Are entrance tickets included?

Your choice, via the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard keeps tickets out - you pay at each gate, only for the places you enter. Exclusive includes the tickets for the listed stops, so the whole day is prepaid.

How do I book and pay?

Pick your date, tap Book, and confirm on WhatsApp. A 10% deposit secures your car and driver; the balance is paid in cash or by transfer at the end of the tour. Guests staying at our villas skip the deposit entirely.

Are the watersport rides included?

On Standard you choose and pay for rides directly at Tanjung Benoa - jet ski, banana boat, parasailing and more. On Exclusive, the watersport session plus GWK and beach tickets are already in the price.

Can you drop us off in the south instead of Ubud?

Of course - staying on in Uluwatu or Jimbaran after the tour is no problem. Just tell us when you book.

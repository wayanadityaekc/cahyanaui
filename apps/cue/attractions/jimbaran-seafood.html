Jimbaran Bay Seafood Dinner

Jimbaran is a fishing bay on Bali's south coast where grilled seafood is served at tables set out on the sand. It follows the sunset Kecak dance, and dinner is paid per dish rather than included.

About Jimbaran Bay

The Bay

A Fishing Village and Its Catch

Jimbaran began as a fishing village on the neck of the Bukit peninsula, and its beachfront cafes grew out of the catch landed each morning at the local market. Fresh fish, prawns, squid and clams are grilled over smouldering coconut husk and brushed with sweet-spicy Balinese sambal, cooked simply and served hot off the coals.

The Setting

Tables on the Sand at the Water's Edge

Tables sit on the beach a few metres from the water, with the bay curving away into the dark and planes climbing out of the nearby airport overhead. As the sun goes down the sky over the sea turns gold and pink, then the candles come out. With your feet in the sand, the setting counts for as much as the food.

Eating Here

Where to Eat, and How It Works

The beachfront is lined with seafood warungs and quality varies, so we point you to a spot where locals eat rather than the tourist rows - honest cooking, fair prices, and fish that's genuinely fresh. Dinner isn't included, so you order what you like and pay as you go. It's a relaxed way to round off a day in the south.

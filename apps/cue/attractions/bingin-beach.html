Bingin Beach

A small, tide-dependent beach near Pecatu, reached down cliffside steps past cafes, with a reef break and a laid-back scene.

The Experience

The Descent

Down Through the Cliffside

Bingin is reached on foot down a maze of steps and paths that wind past cliffside guesthouses, cafes, and surf shops. It's a bit of a scramble, and there's no vehicle access to the sand, which is part of why it keeps its relaxed, low-key feel. The walk down is short but steep.

The Beach

Sand That Comes and Goes

The beach itself changes with the tide: at low water a good stretch of white sand and tide pools opens up, while at high tide the sea comes right in and the sand nearly disappears. Low tide is the time to come for space to sit and swim. Check the tide before you plan your visit.

The Surf & Sunset

Reef Break and Sunset Cafes

Bingin's reef break is a favourite with surfers, best at mid-to-low tide, and fun to watch from the cafes stacked up the cliff. As the day ends, the west-facing beach makes an easy sunset spot - grab a drink, find a step with a view, and watch the light go. It's one of the more social of the quiet Bukit beaches.

Highlights

  • Laid-back cliffside cafe scene
  • Reef break loved by surfers
  • West-facing for sunset
  • Tide pools and white sand at low tide

Before You Go

  • Steep steps down - no vehicle access
  • Best at low tide (sand shrinks at high tide)
  • Bring cash for the cliff cafes
  • Wear shoes you can walk steps in

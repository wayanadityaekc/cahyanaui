Frequently Asked Questions

How does the builder work?

Add tours, activities, and transfers day by day - up to 7 days - then fill in your trip details once and every day follows automatically. The total price updates live as you plan, and one booking covers the whole trip.

Is the price shown final?

It's the live sum of everything you've added, using the same prices as each program page. No fees appear later.

Can I change the itinerary after booking?

Yes - multi-day trips stay flexible. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll adjust the days or programs together.

Is the deposit 10% of the total?

Yes - one 10% deposit locks in every date at once. The balance is settled later in cash or by transfer.

We're changing hotels during the trip - does that work?

Perfectly - each day has its own pick-up and drop-off in the builder, so a trip that moves between areas stays organised.

Still have questions?

Message our local team on WhatsApp - we usually reply within a few hours.

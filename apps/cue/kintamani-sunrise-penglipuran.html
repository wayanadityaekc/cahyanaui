Kintamani Sunrise & Penglipuran Village Tour

Watch the sun rise over an active volcano from a 4x4 jeep, then wind down through the timeless village of Penglipuran, the holy springs of Tirta Empul, and the Tegalalang rice terrace on the way back to Ubud. A private car, a local driver, your pace.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Mount Batur Jeep Sunrise

We set off before dawn so a 4x4 jeep can carry you up the black-lava slopes of Mount Batur to a viewpoint for sunrise over the caldera and lake - no strenuous hike required. Coffee in hand, watch the sky turn as the volcano wakes up. The easiest way to catch Bali's most famous sunrise.

Stop 2

Penglipuran Village

One of the cleanest, best-kept traditional villages in Bali - a Bali Aga village with a single spotless main avenue lined with matching gates and courtyard homes. Step into a family compound and see a way of life that has barely changed in centuries. It sits right on the route down from Batur.

Stop 3

Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple

Bali's most sacred spring temple, where Balinese Hindus have performed the melukat purification ritual for over a thousand years. You're welcome to join the ritual yourself - we'll arrange a sarong and walk you through the etiquette so you can take part with respect.

Stop 4

Tegalalang Rice Terrace & Coffee

We finish at the iconic Tegalalang rice terraces, with a stop at a nearby plantation to taste Balinese coffees and the famous kopi luwak. A relaxed, scenic way to close a very early morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes - every departure is 100% private: your own air-conditioned car with your own friendly English-speaking driver, never shared with other guests. The price is per car for up to 5 people; groups of 6 or more simply take a second car.

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

You choose between two versions with the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard covers the private car, petrol, and your crew - entrance tickets are paid at each stop, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. Exclusive adds the jeep sunrise plus entrance and activity tickets for the listed stops for everyone in your group, so the whole day is prepaid and you never queue at a ticket window.

How long is the tour, and when does it start?

Plan for around 10-11 hours. This is a sunrise trip - pick-up from Ubud is very early so the 4x4 gets you up Mount Batur before first light; we confirm the exact time when you book.

Can we change or skip stops?

Absolutely - the listed stops are a starting point, not a fixed route. Add a stop, swap one out, or stay longer wherever you're enjoying yourself; just tell us before the day or tell your driver on the road.

How do I book, and what do I pay upfront?

Tap the Book button, pick your date, and confirm the details with us on WhatsApp. Only a 10% deposit secures your car and driver - the rest is settled later, so your plans stay flexible.

Do I have to hike for the sunrise?

No - that's the point of this version. A 4x4 jeep drives you up Mount Batur's lava fields to the sunrise viewpoint, so you get the full volcano dawn without the trek. Bring a warm layer; it's chilly before sunrise.

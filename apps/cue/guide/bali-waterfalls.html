The Best Waterfalls in Bali

From easy jungle pools to wild, multi-tier treks.

Bali Guide › Nature

Bali's interior is full of waterfalls, from easy roadside stops to wild jungle treks. They range from wide, powerful curtains of water to tall, narrow drops hidden deep in the rainforest.

Near Ubud, Tegenungan is one of the most accessible, with a natural pool at its base for a swim. In the north, Sekumpul - a cluster of tall falls in a jungle amphitheatre - is often called the most beautiful in Bali, though reaching it means a proper trek.

Many waterfalls have pools you can swim in, making them a refreshing break on a day of sightseeing. Bring a swimsuit, a towel, and shoes with grip.

Planning a Waterfall Trip

Waterfalls are scattered across the island's interior and north, so it's worth grouping a few near each other rather than criss-crossing Bali in a day. Most charge a small entrance fee, and many involve a stairway or short trek down into the valley - and back up again.

Water levels are highest in the rainy season, when the falls are at their most powerful but the trails are muddiest. Go early to have the pools to yourself, and check whether swimming is allowed, as some sacred falls don't permit it.

Good to Know

  • Many have pools you can swim in
  • Trails can be steep & slippery - wear grippy shoes
  • Bring a swimsuit & towel

Uluwatu Cliff Temple

A thousand-year-old sea temple balanced on a cliff 70 metres above the ocean.

About

Pura Luhur Uluwatu is a thousand-year-old sea temple perched on a cliff some 70 metres above the crashing ocean - one of Bali's most important spiritual sites and its most breathtaking coastal view. A clifftop path runs along the edge, giving you the best panorama in South Bali.

The temple is home to a troop of cheeky monkeys famous for snatching sunglasses and hats, so keep your things tucked away. Many visitors pair the temple with the sunset Kecak fire dance held on the cliff nearby.

Highlights

  • Clifftop sea temple 70 m above the ocean
  • The best coastal panorama in South Bali
  • An ancient, important Balinese sea temple
  • Sunset Kecak dance held right on the cliff

Good to Know

  • Sarong required and provided (we arrange it)
  • Watch the monkeys - they grab sunglasses & phones
  • Clifftop paths - mind the edges and the wind

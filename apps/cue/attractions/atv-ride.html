ATV Quad Bike Ride

Mud, jungle, rice fields, river crossings, and a ride straight through a cave tunnel. The messiest, loudest, most fun two hours you'll have in Ubud - and you don't need a licence or any experience.

The Experience

The Ride

Jungle, Mud & a Cave Tunnel

The track runs through working rice paddies, dense jungle, and a shallow river you ride straight across - then into a hand-carved tunnel where the only light is your headlamp. Expect mud. A lot of mud. Wear clothes you're happy to throw away, or take the shower they provide afterwards.

Skill Level

Beginners Absolutely Welcome

No licence, no experience needed. The quads are automatic - twist to go, squeeze to stop. You get a full safety briefing and a practice loop before hitting the trail, and a guide rides with your group the whole way. Choose a single quad, or ride tandem with a partner on one machine.

Good to Know

What to Bring & What's Provided

Helmet, gloves, boots, and knee protection are provided - you just bring clothes you don't mind ruining and a change for after. There are showers, lockers, and a lunch spot at the base. Minimum age is 12 to ride solo. Sessions run morning and afternoon, and it goes ahead in light rain (that's half the fun).

About the Batur Hot Springs

The Hot Springs

Volcanic Water by the Lake

Mineral-rich water, warmed deep underground by Mount Batur, fills a series of pools right on the shore of Lake Batur. Slip in and let the heat work on tired muscles while you look out over the calm water and the caldera walls rising around the crater.

After the Trek

Earn Your Soak

The soak lands best straight after the pre-dawn summit hike. The warm water eases stiff legs and shakes off the cold mountain air, so you finish the morning relaxed rather than wrecked. Most people spend about an hour here before drying off.

Facilities

Bring Your Swimmers

The site has pools of varying temperature, changing rooms, and a lakeside cafe, so ease into the hotter pools gently. Bring swimwear and a towel, or let us know and we'll arrange a towel for you.

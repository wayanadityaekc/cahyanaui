Tour FAQ

Are entrance tickets included in the price?

That's your choice - every tour comes in two versions. On the Standard version, entrance tickets are paid directly at each site, so you only pay for the places you actually enter. The Exclusive version adds the entrance tickets for the listed stops to the price, so the whole day is prepaid. Toggle between the two on any tour page to compare.

How long does a full-day tour take?

Most day tours run 8 to 12 hours, depending on how far the region is and how long you linger. We'll give you a realistic time estimate when you book, and pick-up time is always your call.

Is the tour priced per person or per car?

Per car - up to 5 passengers for one flat price, and every tour is 100% private: you never share the car with strangers. Bring more people and the cost per person drops. For groups above 5 we simply arrange a second car so everyone travels comfortably.

What happens if it rains?

Bali showers usually pass within an hour, so we just reorder the stops to work around the weather. Most tours run rain or shine. If conditions make a stop genuinely unsafe, we'll swap it for something else at no extra charge.

What happens if I visit during Nyepi?

Nyepi is Bali's day of silence (in March; the date shifts each year): for one full day the entire island stops - no traffic, no outings, even the airport closes. Tours can't run that day, but the days around it are fascinating, with the ogoh-ogoh parades the evening before. Tell us your dates and we'll plan around it.

Bali Zoo

Bali Zoo sits in Singapadu, south of Ubud, with elephants, orangutans, sun bears, and hundreds of animals across shaded jungle grounds. It offers optional feeding and encounter experiences and a night safari after dark. Allow two to three hours.

About Bali Zoo

The Animals

Elephants, Orangutans & Jungle Grounds

Open since the late 1990s, Bali Zoo lays out shaded jungle paths past elephants, orangutans, sun bears, big cats, and hundreds of tropical animals in green, garden-like grounds. It is compact enough for a relaxed half-day but has enough to see that children tend not to want to leave.

Up-Close Experiences

Feeding, Encounters & Night Safari

Beyond the walk-through, there are optional add-ons such as animal feeding sessions, an elephant encounter, and breakfast alongside the orangutans. After dark the park runs a night safari, which shows a different side of the grounds as the nocturnal animals become active.

Visiting

A Family Half-Day Out

Paths are shady and mostly easy-going, with cafes and rest spots throughout, so it suits all ages. Allow around two to three hours for a day visit. Bali Bird Park is next door, so the two combine easily into a full wildlife day out from Ubud.

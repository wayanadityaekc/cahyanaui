South Coast & Uluwatu Sunset Kecak

Clifftop temples, white-sand southern beaches, and the spellbinding Kecak fire dance as the sun sinks into the Indian Ocean at Uluwatu - the perfect afternoon-to-evening escape from Ubud.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Padang Padang Beach

A small cove reached by squeezing down a staircase through a gap in the rocks - famous from Eat Pray Love, loved by surfers, and a beautiful pocket of sand framed by cliffs and clear water.

Stop 2

Uluwatu Clifftop Temple

One of Bali's six key spiritual pillars, perched on a cliff 70 metres above the crashing surf. Walk the clifftop path for jaw-dropping ocean views - and keep an eye on your belongings, the resident monkeys are famously cheeky.

Stop 3

Sunset Kecak Fire Dance

As the sun sets, more than seventy chanting men form a circle of sound around a ring of fire, retelling the Ramayana in one of Bali's most electrifying performances. With the ocean and sky ablaze behind it, it's pure magic.

Stop 4

Jimbaran Seafood Dinner (optional)

End the night with your toes in the sand at Jimbaran Bay, where grills line the beach and the day's fresh catch is cooked to order. Optional and paid on the spot - but a hard one to resist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car - one price covers up to 5 people, so a fuller car costs less per head. Groups of 6 or more simply take a second car. Only the Exclusive version adds a per-person part: the entrance tickets.

Are entrance tickets included?

Your choice, via the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard keeps tickets out - you pay at each gate, only for the places you enter. Exclusive includes the tickets for the listed stops, so the whole day is prepaid.

How do I book and pay?

Pick your date, tap Book, and confirm on WhatsApp. A 10% deposit secures your car and driver; the balance is paid in cash or by transfer at the end of the tour. Guests staying at our villas skip the deposit entirely.

What time do we get back?

The sunset show ends around 7 PM; with the optional Jimbaran dinner you're back in Ubud around 10. This is deliberately a late day.

Is the Jimbaran seafood dinner included?

No - it's an optional finale. You pick the day's fresh catch at the beachside grills and pay on the spot. Tell your driver during the day and the timing is arranged around it.

Our Story

Who We Are

Born and Raised in Ubud

Cahyana Ubud Experience started with one car, one driver, and a simple idea: that visitors deserve to see Bali the way locals actually live it. It has grown since then - a few more drivers, and the villas over at ubudprivatevillas.com - but it is still the same family. We're not an agency with a call centre. The same people who answer your message are the ones who show up at your hotel in the morning.

How We Work

Plan It Once, in One Place

Most Bali trips get stitched together from a dozen browser tabs - one site for the tour, another for the airport pickup, a third for somewhere to sleep, each with its own price and its own person to chase. Here it's one conversation. Tell us how many days you have and what you want to see, and we build the whole thing around you. Nothing gets locked in either: want to stay longer at a waterfall, skip a temple, or stop for coffee at a warung you spotted from the car? Just say so on the day.

What We Believe

Honest Prices, No Games

Every price is already on this website. You shouldn't have to message a stranger and wait a day just to find out what a Tuesday costs. And the price you see is the price you pay: no commission stops at overpriced souvenir shops, no surprise fees on the day, no pressure to buy anything. Just a small 10% deposit to confirm your booking, and you settle the rest at the end - and if you're staying at one of our villas, you pay nothing upfront at all. If that sounds fair, you haven't booked many tours in Bali.

Why travel with us

Because the difference between a good day in Bali and a forgettable one is almost never the destination - it's who takes you there, how well they know it, and whether they told you the real price before you got in the car.

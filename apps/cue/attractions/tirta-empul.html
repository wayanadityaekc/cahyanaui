Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple

Bali's sacred spring temple, home to a thousand-year-old purification ritual.

About

Tirta Empul is one of Bali's most revered temples, built around a natural spring where Balinese Hindus have performed the melukat water-purification ritual for more than a thousand years. Beyond the bathing pools you'll find peaceful courtyards, koi ponds, and finely carved stone shrines. Visitors are welcome to take part in the ritual - your guide arranges a sarong and walks you through the etiquette so you can join with confidence and respect.

For the Balinese, coming here is deeply personal - families arrive to cleanse body and mind at the row of stone spouts, each fountain carrying its own meaning. Stepping into the line yourself, cool spring water running over your hands, turns the visit from sightseeing into something you actually take part in - a gentle way to experience a tradition that's still very much alive.

Highlights

  • Sacred natural spring dating back over 1,000 years
  • Join the melukat purification ritual (optional)
  • Beautiful courtyards, koi ponds & carved stone gates
  • Sarong provided; guide explains the customs

Good to Know

  • Modest dress required - sarong & sash (we arrange it)
  • Bring a change of clothes & a towel if you enter the water
  • It's an active place of worship - follow your guide's lead

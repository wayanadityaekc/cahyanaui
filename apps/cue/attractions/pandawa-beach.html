Pandawa Beach

A once-secret beach reached through a dramatic cut in the cliff lined with carved statues.

About

Pandawa was once called "Secret Beach" because it was hidden behind a limestone ridge. Today you reach it through a dramatic cut in the cliff lined with giant carved statues, opening onto calm water, wide sand, and space to actually breathe.

It's a good swim stop, more relaxed than the busier southern beaches, with loungers, kayaks, and gentle water. The statue-lined approach makes for a memorable arrival.

Highlights

  • Dramatic cliff-cut entrance lined with statues
  • Calm water and a wide, open beach
  • More relaxed than the busier south beaches
  • Good for swimming and kayaking

Good to Know

  • Bring a swimsuit & towel for the water
  • A small entrance/parking fee usually applies
  • Loungers and kayaks are available to rent

Git Git Waterfall

A 35-metre rainforest waterfall reached by an easy paved path through coffee and clove trees.

About

Git Git is a 35-metre single-drop waterfall crashing into a pool deep in the rainforest, reached by an easy paved path through coffee and clove trees. It's far gentler to reach than Sekumpul, making it a good place to cool off without a serious hike.

The walk in is shaded and pretty, passing spice trees and small plantations. At the base, the spray and the roar of the falls make a refreshing pause on the northern route.

Highlights

  • A tall 35 m rainforest waterfall
  • Easy paved path - no hard trek
  • Walk through coffee & clove plantations
  • A refreshing, accessible stop

Good to Know

  • Paths can be slippery - wear good shoes
  • A small entrance fee usually applies
  • Bring water; it's an uphill walk back

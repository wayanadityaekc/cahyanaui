3-Day Best of Bali Package: Ubud, Kintamani & Uluwatu

A private multi-day route through Bali's three signature areas - Ubud's temples and rice terraces, the Kintamani highlands and Besakih Mother Temple, then Uluwatu's cliffs and sunset Kecak. Same driver and car for all three days.

  • Duration 3 days / 2 nights
  • Area Ubud, Kintamani & Uluwatu
  • Group Up to 6 guests

What You'll Do

Day 1 · Ubud

Stop 1

Tegalalang Rice Terrace

The iconic stepped rice fields north of Ubud, hand-carved over generations and still fed by Bali's thousand year old subak irrigation system.
Stop 2

Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary

A moss-covered jungle temple complex in the heart of Ubud, home to more than 700 long-tailed macaques. Overnight in Ubud.

Day 2 · Kintamani & Besakih

Stop 3

Kintamani & Mount Batur Viewpoint

A highland lookout over the still-active Mount Batur volcano and its crater lake, with cool mountain air and coffee-growing hillsides. A relaxed roadside stop, not the pre-dawn trek.
Stop 4

Besakih Mother Temple

Bali's largest and holiest temple complex, terraced up the slopes of Mount Agung. Overnight near Uluwatu.

Day 3 · Uluwatu

Stop 5

Uluwatu Temple

A clifftop temple 70 metres above the surf on Bali's southern tip, with resident macaques and sweeping ocean views.
Stop 6

Sunset Kecak Fire Dance

A traditional Kecak performance at the clifftop amphitheater as the sun sets over the ocean - a fitting close to the trip, before drop-off.

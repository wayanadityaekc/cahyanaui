Ubud Tour

Bali's cultural heart in one unforgettable day — sacred temples, emerald rice terraces, jungle waterfalls, and the famous Monkey Forest. A private car, a local guide, and an itinerary that bends to your pace.

What You'll Visit

Stop 1

Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary

A moss-covered jungle temple complex in the heart of Ubud, home to more than 700 long-tailed macaques. Walk beneath ancient banyan roots and past weathered stone guardians in one of Bali's most atmospheric sanctuaries. Your guide will show you how to keep your belongings safe and interact with the monkeys respectfully.

Stop 2

Tegalalang Rice Terrace

The iconic stepped rice fields north of Ubud, hand-carved over generations and still fed by Bali's thousand-year-old subak irrigation system — a UNESCO-recognised tradition. We time this stop for the best light and the smallest crowds, so you get the view without the queue.

Stop 3

Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple

Bali's most sacred spring temple, where Balinese Hindus have performed the melukat purification ritual for over a thousand years. You're welcome to join the ritual yourself — we'll arrange a sarong and walk you through the etiquette so you can take part with confidence and respect.

Stop 4

Tegenungan Waterfall

A wide, powerful waterfall tucked into the jungle just outside Ubud, with a natural pool at its base you can swim in. It's one of the few major Bali waterfalls with easy access — a short walk down, and a genuinely refreshing reward after a morning of temples.

Stop 5

Coffee Plantation & Bali Swing

Walk through a working Balinese plantation, see how cacao, vanilla, and coffee grow, and taste the famous kopi luwak straight from the source. Tastings are complimentary. If you're feeling brave, finish by soaring out over the jungle valley on the Bali swing.

Stop 6

Campuhan Ridge Walk

A gentle green ridge trail running between two rivers, flanked by tall elephant grass and rolling hills. It's Ubud's most peaceful walk — no entrance fee, no crowds, and the best light of the whole day if we get here in the late afternoon.

Stop 7

Ubud Art Market & Royal Palace

Handwoven baskets, batik, silver, and wood carvings sold by the artisans who made them — plus the royal Puri Saren Palace right across the street. Bring cash, and let your guide help you bargain fairly so both you and the seller walk away happy.

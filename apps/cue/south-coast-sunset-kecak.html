South Bali & Sunset Kecak

The whole southern peninsula in one day - watersports at Tanjung Benoa, the giant GWK statue, Pandawa beach, the clifftop Uluwatu temple, and the Kecak fire dance as the sun sets over the Indian Ocean.

What You'll Do

Stop 1

Watersports at Tanjung Benoa

Start the day out on the water at Tanjung Benoa, Bali's watersport hub. Pick from jet ski, banana boat, parasailing, or a glass-bottom boat over to Turtle Island. Gear and instructors are on hand, so first-timers are welcome. A fun, splashy way to kick things off.

Stop 2

Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK)

One of the tallest statues on earth - Lord Vishnu riding the mythical Garuda, standing 121 metres above a limestone cultural park. Taller than the Statue of Liberty, and it took 28 years to finish. Impressive up close in a way photos never quite capture.

Stop 3

Pandawa Beach

Once called "Secret Beach" because it was hidden behind a limestone ridge, now reached through a dramatic cut in the cliff lined with giant carved statues. Calm water, wide sand, and space to actually breathe. A good swim stop before the afternoon.

Stop 4

Uluwatu Clifftop Temple & Sunset Kecak

One of Bali's six key spiritual pillars, perched on a cliff 70 metres above the crashing surf. Walk the clifftop path for wide-open ocean views - and keep an eye on your belongings, the resident monkeys are famously cheeky. We stay on for the Kecak in the temple's cliff-edge amphitheatre: more than seventy chanting men around a ring of fire, retelling the Ramayana with the sunset over the ocean right behind the stage.

Stop 5

Jimbaran Seafood Dinner (optional)

End the night with your toes in the sand at Jimbaran Bay, where grills line the beach and the day's fresh catch is cooked to order. Optional and paid on the spot - but a hard one to resist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car - one price covers up to 5 people, so a fuller car costs less per head. Groups of 6 or more simply take a second car. Only the Exclusive version adds a per-person part: the entrance tickets and watersport package.

Are entrance tickets included?

Your choice, via the Standard / Exclusive toggle above. Standard keeps tickets out - you pay at each gate, only for the places you enter. Exclusive includes the watersport package plus the tickets for the listed stops, so the whole day is prepaid.

How do I book and pay?

Pick your date, tap Book, and confirm on WhatsApp. A 10% deposit secures your car and driver; the balance is paid in cash or by transfer at the end of the tour. Guests staying at our villas skip the deposit entirely.

How long is this day?

It's a full day - a mid-morning start out to the coast, then watersports, GWK, and the beach through the afternoon, ending with Uluwatu and the sunset Kecak show around 7 PM. With the optional Jimbaran dinner you're back in Ubud around 10.

Is the Jimbaran seafood dinner included?

No - it's an optional finale. You pick the day's fresh catch at the beachside grills and pay on the spot. Tell your driver during the day and the timing is arranged around it.

Lovina Dolphin Sunrise

A dawn boat trip from Lovina's black-sand coast to see wild spinner dolphins.

The Experience

The Departure

Out on the Water Before Dawn

While it's still dark, a traditional outrigger jukung pushes off from Lovina's black-sand beach and heads out onto the calm Bali Sea. The water on the north coast is glassy and gentle at this hour, nothing like the surf of the south. As the sky begins to lighten, dozens of small boats fan out across the bay to search for the pods.

The Dolphins

Wild Spinner Dolphins at Sunrise

Within about half an hour you're often surrounded by wild spinner dolphins racing the swell, leaping and rolling alongside the boat. They are truly wild, so sightings are common rather than guaranteed - but Lovina is one of the most reliable places in Bali to see them. Watching them play as the sun comes up is a magical, only-in-the-north experience.

The North Coast

A Quieter Side of Bali

Lovina sits far from the busy south, on a stretch of black volcanic sand backed by mountains. Because the trip starts so early and lies a long way from Ubud, it works best paired with an overnight stay in the north. Just ask and we'll build the itinerary around it, so you wake up already on the coast.
